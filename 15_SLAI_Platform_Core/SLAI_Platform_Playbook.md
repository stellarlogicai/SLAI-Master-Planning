# SLAI Platform Playbook

Status: Company-wide planning standard

Purpose: Define the reusable product, business, UX, security, and operating principles shared across Stellar Logic AI software divisions.

## Core UX Standard

See [SLAI UX Philosophy](SLAI_UX_Philosophy.md).

The central design rule is:

> **Learn in minutes. Master through use.**

Supporting principles:

> **Complex engineering. Simple experiences.**

> **Power without complexity.**

Every SLAI product should guide the user through the correct workflow rather than requiring the user to memorize menus, system structure, or hidden process rules.

## Shared Product Pattern

Each SLAI software division may eventually reuse or adapt:

- multi-tenant architecture,
- tenant and role isolation,
- guided workflows,
- AI usage and credits,
- quote or subscription billing,
- audit logs,
- notifications and exception handling,
- implementation services,
- training and onboarding,
- partner integrations,
- reporting and analytics,
- security and compliance controls,
- customer success,
- and human approval for important AI-assisted decisions.

## Company-Wide Product Rules

1. AI amplifies people rather than replacing human responsibility.
2. Build simple first; complexity must be earned.
3. Internal complexity is acceptable when it creates customer simplicity.
4. MVPs must solve real operational problems.
5. Data informs decisions; qualified humans interpret and approve important actions.
6. Workflows should be predictable, stable, role-specific, and difficult to misuse.
7. New features must reduce steps, confusion, cost, risk, or decision burden.
8. Products should standardize approved processes while preserving human authority to improve those standards.
9. Every major workflow must have measurable acceptance criteria.
10. ServicesOS remains the active product priority until its promotion gates are met.

## Software Division Model

Stellar Logic AI operates as one company with specialized software divisions.

Examples include:

- ServicesOS Division,
- GrowOS Division,
- EducationOS Division,
- RetailOS / PharmacyOS Division,
- ComplianceAI Division,
- and future divisions.

Shared internal intelligence systems support those divisions:

### SLAIOS

- preserves company knowledge,
- records decisions,
- tracks priorities,
- packages context,
- supports team handoffs,
- and helps keep divisions aligned.

### GrowthAI

- researches markets,
- identifies opportunities,
- scores leads,
- supports approved outreach,
- tracks objections,
- and helps divisions grow through evidence rather than guesswork.

## Reusable Launch Pattern

A future SLAI division should generally progress through:

1. Problem discovery
2. Market validation
3. Narrow product wedge
4. Architecture and safety boundaries
5. Internal testing
6. Stable customer-ready V1
7. Controlled customer deployment
8. Measured ROI
9. Repeatable onboarding
10. Team-supported growth

No division should skip directly from an exciting idea to a broad build.

## UX Acceptance Standard

A frontline employee with light role knowledge should be able to perform the basic workflow after a short walkthrough.

The interface should answer:

- What should I do next?
- What is most important?
- What information do I need?
- Am I finished?

A workflow is not complete if users still need to ask:

- Where is the feature?
- Which menu is it under?
- Which version of the process is correct?
- What happens if I click this?

## Operational Consistency Standard

SLAI products should help organizations create one approved, measurable operating method.

> **Standardize the process. Measure the execution. Improve the standard.**

The platform should preserve:

- SOP versions,
- assignments,
- completion evidence,
- exceptions,
- approvals,
- outcomes,
- and decision history.

## AI Governance Standard

Across all SLAI divisions:

- AI notices,
- AI compares,
- AI explains,
- AI suggests,
- humans approve important decisions,
- and the system records what happened.

AI should never silently take ownership of a material human decision.

## Future Expansion

This playbook should grow only when a pattern has been proven through a real SLAI product.

ServicesOS is the first major proving ground. Reusable lessons should be documented after they work in production rather than prematurely abstracted into a large shared platform.
