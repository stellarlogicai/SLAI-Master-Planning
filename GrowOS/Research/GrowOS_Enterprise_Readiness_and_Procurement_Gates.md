# GrowOS Enterprise Readiness and Procurement Gates

## Purpose
This document captures the enterprise adoption requirements identified during market research.

## Key Principle
Enterprise readiness is a later phase. GrowOS V1 should target design partners and mid-size operators while being architected so enterprise capabilities can be added without major redesign.

## Enterprise Readiness Tracks
### Customer Ready
- Stable multi-tenant architecture
- Simple employee workflows
- Audit logging
- Backups
- Role-based permissions
- Data ownership protections
- Read-only integrations
- Manual fallback procedures

### Enterprise Ready
- METRC validated integration
- SOC 2 readiness, then certification when justified
- External penetration testing
- Disaster recovery and business continuity
- Enterprise SLAs
- Security questionnaires
- Enterprise contracts
- OT-safe architecture and segmentation
- Proven customer references and ROI case studies

## Procurement Philosophy
GrowOS should preserve existing hardware, integrate with METRC rather than replace it, remain read-only around operational technology until validated, and keep complex security and compliance mechanisms behind a simple user experience.

## Strategic Conclusion
The enterprise gatekeepers are planning milestones, not reasons to delay V1. ServicesOS remains priority one. GrowOS should continue partner discovery and planning until ServicesOS reaches the previously defined stability and staffing milestones.
