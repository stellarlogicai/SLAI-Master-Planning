# GrowOS Planning

**Status:** Advanced discovery and future product planning  
**Active build priority:** ServicesOS remains priority one.

## Purpose

This folder is the canonical home for GrowOS / GreenhouseOS planning, commercial research, partner discovery, enterprise readiness, AI/data architecture, workflow design, and future product decisions.

## Core Direction

GrowOS should become the cultivation workflow and intelligence operating layer for commercial growers.

The current strategy is:

- keep METRC as the regulated system of record,
- preserve existing hardware where practical,
- standardize facility-approved workflows,
- make employee execution simple and role-specific,
- consolidate fragmented operations and reporting,
- begin with read-only integrations,
- require human approval for important decisions,
- and delay controller write access or autonomous control until much later.
