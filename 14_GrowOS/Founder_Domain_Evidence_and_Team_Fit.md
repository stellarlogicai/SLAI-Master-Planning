## 3. Harvest-to-Reload and Room Reset Expertise

Jamie's principal ownership area was the room-reset lifecycle:

```text
Harvest
  -> room unload
  -> waste and material handling
  -> sanitation and cleaning
  -> inspection and readiness work
  -> equipment and room reset
  -> plant loading
  -> room handoff to cultivation
```

Jamie experimented with sequencing, labor use, process design, and standardization. The prior process took approximately one week. Jamie reduced the turnaround to approximately three days.

That represents an estimated reduction of about 57% in room-reset cycle time.

### Operational significance

A faster, controlled room turnaround can influence:

- room downtime,
- production scheduling,
- labor utilization,
- contamination risk,
- readiness consistency,
- timing of the next crop cycle,
- and annual facility throughput.

This is direct evidence that Jamie has already performed the type of workflow analysis GrowOS is intended to support:

> observe the workflow, identify wasted time and failure points, test alternatives, standardize the better process, train the team, track execution, and measure the outcome.

## 9. Does This Change the Deep-Research Conclusion?

It strengthens the opportunity, but it does not change the core recommendation.

### What becomes stronger

- Founder-market fit is substantially better than the report assumed.
- The team may already cover technical, cultivation, workflow, training, compliance, market-access, and capital capabilities.
- Access to realistic SOPs, commercial workflows, and industry interviews may be easier.
- Pilot recruitment may be more credible.
- The risk of building an outsider's idea of cultivation software is lower.
- Jamie's room-reset case provides an early internal proof point for workflow optimization.

### What does not change

- ServicesOS remains priority one.
- GrowOS still requires independent validation beyond this founding group.
- The first wedge should remain SOP execution and anomaly visibility.
- METRC certification and integrations remain non-trivial.
- Hardware-heavy and autonomous-control scope should remain delayed.
- Equity should not be decided from potential contribution alone.
- A related concept from Budd creates an intellectual-property question that must be handled before combined development.

### Revised interpretation

The deep-research rating remains:

> **Promising but requires more discovery.**

But the confidence that SLAI could credibly execute the opportunity increases because the possible team has unusually relevant operating history and complementary capabilities.
