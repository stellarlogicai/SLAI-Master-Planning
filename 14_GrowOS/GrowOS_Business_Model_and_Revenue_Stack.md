# GrowOS Business Model and Revenue Stack

Status: Future division planning / no active build authorization

## 1. Division Structure

GrowOS is planned as a future **software division of Stellar Logic AI**, not automatically as a separate company.

The division may have its own:

- product leadership,
- engineering,
- cultivation consulting,
- implementation,
- sales,
- customer success,
- support,
- budget,
- roadmap,
- and revenue targets.

Shared SLAI capabilities may support GrowOS over time, including billing, AI credits, security, analytics, training infrastructure, and internal company systems.

A possible cultivation partner may become a founding partner or operating leader for the GrowOS division without becoming a co-founder of Stellar Logic AI itself.

## 2. Core Business Principle

> **Consulting discovers and improves the operation. GrowOS standardizes, measures, and preserves the improved process. Recurring software revenue remains the scalable center of the business.**

Consulting should support product adoption and customer outcomes. It should not trap the division in selling only labor hours.

## 3. Revenue Stack

### GrowOS SaaS

Recurring revenue from:

- facility subscriptions,
- room or scale tiers,
- workflow modules,
- reporting and analytics,
- METRC-related integrations,
- sensor and camera integrations,
- enterprise security and administration,
- and multi-site management.

Most commercial deployments are expected to be quote-based.

### AI Credits

The subscription may include a defined monthly AI allowance. Additional credits may be purchased at a controlled margin.

Credits may cover:

- plant image analysis,
- room-health reasoning,
- anomaly investigations,
- historical comparisons,
- cultivar analysis,
- executive summaries,
- and on-demand manager requests.

Routine monitoring estimates should be included in the quote based on room count, camera count, capture cadence, analysis depth, and retention.

## 4. Consultant Conversion Model

A cultivation consultant may become an effective channel when GrowOS helps preserve the systems they design.

Example:

1. Consultant helps launch or improve a facility.
2. Consultant defines SOPs, workflows, and operating targets.
3. GrowOS digitizes those workflows.
4. Employees execute inside GrowOS.
5. Management receives measurable reporting.
6. The facility retains the operating system after the consulting engagement.

Possible consultant compensation:

- referral bonus,
- implementation compensation,
- limited revenue share for customers actively brought and supported,
- or portfolio pricing for consulting firms.

Referral compensation, consulting work, capital contributions, and co-founder equity must remain separate categories.

## 5. Hardware Compatibility Program

Potential labels:

### GrowOS Compatible

The device or system can exchange supported data with GrowOS under documented conditions.

### GrowOS Verified

SLAI has tested the integration against defined functional, reliability, and security requirements.

### GrowOS Preferred

The product is verified, commercially partnered, and supported as a recommended deployment option.

Commercial partnership must never be confused with technical compatibility. Hardware recommendations should prioritize facility fit and customer value.

## 7. Savings-Based Sales Case

The sales case should not depend on reducing headcount.

A modeled annual savings range such as $20,000-$60,000 for a mid-size facility may equal one or more employee salaries, but the preferred value proposition is:

- remove avoidable administrative work,
- redirect skilled employees toward plant care and production,
- reduce overtime and management chase-work,
- shorten room downtime,
- eliminate duplicate subscriptions,
- improve execution consistency,
- and reduce preventable loss.

GrowOS should amplify the cultivation team rather than position itself primarily as an employee-replacement system.
