## Company-Wide Platform Pricing Rule

Unless Jamie explicitly approves and documents a product-specific exception, the standard base subscription for customer-facing SLAI software platforms is:

```text
$100/month
or
$1,000/year
```

The annual option saves $200 per year and is equivalent to two months free.

This applies by default to ServicesOS, SLAI Web, GrowthAI, EducationOS, RetailOS / PharmacyOS, ComplianceAI, FutureAI customer-facing products, and future SLAI customer-facing platforms when they are actually launched.

The base subscription does not automatically absorb genuinely variable or separate costs such as payment processing, high-volume messaging, AI/provider usage, marketplace fees, hardware, custom implementation, bespoke integrations, or enterprise-only requirements.

See `SLAI_Platform_Pricing_Standard.md` for the authoritative company-wide rule.

## Priority Protection

ServicesOS remains the active product priority.

SLAI Web is a future/supporting revenue and customer-acquisition lane. It should finance and feed ServicesOS rather than consume the engineering focus required to finish ServicesOS V1.

The pricing standard does not promote any parked product into active development.
