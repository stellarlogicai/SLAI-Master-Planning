# SLAI Founder Time Tracking

**Status:** Active internal operating record  
**Effective:** 2026-09-28  
**Owner:** Jamie Brown  
**Purpose:** Track founder time invested in Stellar Logic AI without creating a heavyweight clocking system before SLAIOS exists.

## Core Rule

The **ChatGPT Project named `SLAI MASTER PLANNING` is the time-tracking boundary.**

Jamie keeps SLAI-related ChatGPT work inside this Project. Therefore:

> **Active time spent in any conversation inside the `SLAI MASTER PLANNING` ChatGPT Project counts as SLAI founder work time.**

This avoids having to classify every individual conversation by whether it is "work." If it is intentionally being handled inside the SLAI MASTER PLANNING Project, it is treated as SLAI work.

This can include:
- ServicesOS
- SLAI Web
- SLAI Platform Core
- ServicesOS V2
- SLAIOS / Forge
- other documented SLAI products when intentionally being planned
- architecture and engineering
- testing and QA
- product planning
- company strategy and operations
- research directly connected to SLAI
- documentation
- pricing and business-model work
- customer/sales preparation
- business networking, relationship-building, and partnership development
- infrastructure planning
- founder brainstorming that materially contributes to SLAI

Chats outside the `SLAI MASTER PLANNING` Project are not automatically counted.

## Session Rule

Until SLAI has a formal timekeeping system, ChatGPT-assisted work should be recorded as work sessions.

For a session:
1. Record the date.
2. Record a reasonable start and end time when known.
3. Record the primary project and work type when useful.
4. Record actual duration when reasonably known.
5. If exact duration is not available, record an estimate and mark it as estimated.
6. Do not inflate time for long unattended gaps. Split sessions when there was a meaningful break.
7. Time spent actively directing, reviewing, testing, brainstorming, deciding, documenting, networking, or responding to AI-assisted work inside the `SLAI MASTER PLANNING` Project counts as founder work.
8. Unattended AI execution time does not automatically count as founder labor unless Jamie was actively working on another SLAI task during that period.
9. A conversation may span multiple SLAI topics. It can be logged as `Multi-Project / Company` when one primary project is not meaningful.

## Record Format

The companion file `SLAI_Founder_Time_Log.csv` is the working ledger.

Required fields:
- Date
- Start
- End
- Hours
- Project
- Work Type
- Source
- Estimated
- Notes

`Source` may be values such as ChatGPT, Codex, GitHub, Manual, Customer, or Mixed.

For ChatGPT-derived entries, `SLAI MASTER PLANNING` is the authoritative Project boundary.

## Why Track This

This ledger is intended to help SLAI understand:
- founder effort invested before revenue
- where founder time is actually being spent
- ServicesOS development cost in human effort
- whether future products become faster to build as shared architecture and processes mature
- when founder workload becomes a hiring signal
- whether SLAIOS / Forge actually reduce engineering coordination and repetitive work

It is an internal management record. It should not automatically be treated as payroll, reimbursable wages, or a tax deduction without appropriate accounting/legal treatment.

## Future State

Do not build a dedicated timekeeping product merely to replace this file.

When SLAIOS reaches the appropriate stage, this simple ledger can inform a proper company time/work tracking capability. Until then, simple and consistent tracking is preferred over additional product scope.

## Priority Protection

Time tracking must not become a distraction from ServicesOS V1.

The tracker exists to record work, not create more work.
