# SLAI Platform Pricing Standard

**Status:** Authoritative company-wide pricing policy  
**Created:** 2026-09-15  
**Owner:** Jamie Brown / Stellar Logic AI

## Purpose

Define one simple default subscription price across Stellar Logic AI customer-facing software platforms so pricing stays easy to understand, easy to sell, and consistent across the company.

> **Default SLAI platform subscription: $100/month or $1,000/year.**

The annual plan provides the same normal product entitlement as monthly billing and saves the customer $200 per year, equivalent to two months free.

---

## 1. Company-Wide Default

For standard customer-facing SLAI software platforms, the default base subscription is:

```text
Monthly
$100 / month

Annual
$1,000 / year
= $200 annual savings
= 2 months free
= ~$83.33/month effective
```

Monthly and annual customers receive the same normal platform capability set unless a future product explicitly defines a separate paid add-on that is justified by real cost or value.

Annual pricing is a billing-frequency/commitment discount, not a lesser or greater product tier.

---

## 2. Products Covered by Default

This pricing standard applies by default when these customer-facing platforms are commercially launched:

- ServicesOS
- SLAI Web
- GrowthAI
- EducationOS
- RetailOS / PharmacyOS
- ComplianceAI
- FutureAI customer-facing products
- future SLAI customer-facing software platforms unless Jamie explicitly approves a different model

This document does not activate or promote any parked product. Product priority remains governed by the SLAI Master Planning priority rules.

SLAIOS is an internal company operating system and is not automatically treated as a customer subscription product.

---

## 3. Product Independence

Each SLAI platform keeps visible standalone value.

Default rule:

```text
1 subscribed SLAI platform = $100/month or $1,000/year
2 subscribed SLAI platforms = $200/month or $2,000/year
3 subscribed SLAI platforms = $300/month or $3,000/year
```

There is no automatic multi-product bundle discount in the base policy.

Jamie may later approve bundles or cross-product promotions if real customer behavior shows they improve conversion or retention without weakening product economics.

---

## 4. What the Standard Price Does Not Automatically Include

The $100/month or $1,000/year base subscription does not mean SLAI must absorb every genuinely variable or exceptional cost.

The following may remain separately governed when applicable:

- Stripe/payment processing fees,
- SMS/telephony/high-volume messaging,
- provider-backed AI credits or unusually high model usage,
- exceptional storage/bandwidth/traffic,
- marketplace transaction fees,
- ecommerce/payment marketplace fees,
- hardware or device costs,
- unusual third-party API costs,
- custom implementation work,
- custom integrations,
- professional services,
- bespoke enterprise/security/compliance work,
- unusually high-support or dedicated-infrastructure requirements.

Any separate charge should be transparent, attributable, and justified by real cost or clearly separate scope.

Do not use variable-cost protection as an excuse to recreate confusing feature-tier pricing.

---

## 5. One-Time Services Are Separate

A platform subscription is separate from one-time implementation or professional-service work.

Examples:

```text
SLAI Web done-for-you build
one-time implementation fee
+
$100/month or $1,000/year platform subscription
```

The same principle applies to future SLAI products that require setup, migration, custom configuration, hardware deployment, training, or bespoke implementation.

A one-time payment never creates lifetime platform entitlement unless Jamie explicitly approves and documents an exception.

---

## 6. Default Pricing Philosophy

SLAI should favor:

- simple pricing,
- transparent pricing,
- strong value relative to cost,
- no artificial feature fragmentation,
- no aggressive per-seat penalties for normal customer growth unless real cost requires them,
- annual discounts that reward commitment,
- explicit variable-cost safeguards,
- real-world validation before adding complexity.

Core rule:

> **Keep the standard platform price simple. Complexity must be earned by demonstrated customer need or real cost exposure.**

---

## 7. Exceptions

A product may deviate from the $100/month / $1,000/year standard only when Jamie explicitly approves an exception based on evidence such as:

- enterprise-only sales model,
- materially higher infrastructure cost,
- regulated/compliance burden,
- dedicated hardware or deployment requirements,
- materially different support burden,
- unusually high transaction volume,
- contractual/security obligations,
- economics that make the standard price unsustainable.

An exception must be documented in that product's pricing plan and should explain why the company-wide default does not fit.

A product being more advanced or having more features is not, by itself, enough reason to abandon the standard.

---

## 8. Promotions and Design Partners

Temporary promotions, founder/design-partner rates, credits, or comped periods may be approved by Jamie.

They should:

- preserve the visible standard list price,
- have a defined reason,
- have a defined duration or end condition where practical,
- remain auditable,
- not silently become permanent pricing,
- keep variable-cost exposure bounded.

---

## 9. Billing Architecture Direction

Where practical, customer-facing SLAI platforms should share the same basic commercial pattern:

```text
monthly price
or
annual price
        ↓
verified payment-provider state
        ↓
canonical product entitlement
        ↓
role/permission/site-state checks
        ↓
allowed product actions
```

Product-specific billing details may differ, but the visible base subscription should remain consistent unless an approved exception exists.

---

## 10. Current Authority

This company-wide pricing standard supersedes older planning that proposed arbitrary starter/growth/professional platform tiers or differing base subscription prices solely because a product or vertical was different.

Current authoritative default:

> **All standard customer-facing SLAI software platforms: $100/month or $1,000/year.**

Two months free is the standard annual-billing discount.
