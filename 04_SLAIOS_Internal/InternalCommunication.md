# SLAIOS Internal Communication

## Purpose

For a primarily remote company, SLAIOS communication should be part of the operating system rather than a disconnected stream of messages.

Communication should connect people, work, decisions, products, and company memory.

## Core Surfaces

Potential structure:

- company announcements,
- product channels,
- department/team channels,
- project channels,
- DMs,
- small groups,
- decision threads,
- handoffs,
- and optional social/culture spaces.

## Async First

SLAIOS should reduce the need for everyone to be online at the same time.

Capabilities may include:

- time-zone-aware notifications,
- "catch me up" summaries,
- messages needing response,
- daily/weekly handoffs,
- decision summaries,
- linked tasks/projects,
- and clear ownership.

## Role-Aware AI Summaries

An employee should be able to ask:

> Catch me up on what changed while I was offline.

Luna may summarize only communication and work that employee is authorized to access.

No summary may leak restricted channels, HR information, compensation, executive strategy, or unrelated product data.

## Decision Capture

Important discussions should be convertible into durable decision records.

Example:

```text
Discussion
  |
Record Decision
  |
Decision:
GrowthAI V1 will not auto-publish social content.

Reason:
Human approval is required before consequential external action.

Owner:
Authorized product decision maker

Related:
ServicesOS / GrowthAI
```

Company memory should link back to the source discussion where policy permits.

## Communication to Work

A message may become:

- task,
- bug,
- product idea,
- decision,
- handoff,
- incident,
- suggestion,
- or follow-up.

SLAIOS should preserve the source and contributor rather than silently converting conversation into unattributed work.

## Handoffs

Remote employees should be able to create or approve structured handoffs:

- completed,
- remaining,
- current blocker,
- next owner,
- relevant links/context,
- urgency,
- expected next action.

AI may prepare the handoff; the employee reviews it.

## No Surveillance

Internal communication must not become an employee-scoring feed.

Do not use:

- message count,
- response speed,
- sentiment,
- time online,
- or chat activity

as hidden productivity or performance scores.

Communication evidence may support a factual contribution only when it clearly documents actual work and follows the contribution-record rules.

## Search and Retention

Search should respect the same authorization boundaries as direct viewing.

Retention and legal/compliance policy should be defined before external commercialization.
