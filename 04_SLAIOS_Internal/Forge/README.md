# SLAIOS Forge

Status: **Parked future SLAIOS add-on.**

## What Forge Is

Forge is the engineering execution add-on for SLAIOS.

SLAIOS understands:

- the company,
- business priorities,
- employees,
- permissions,
- products,
- repositories,
- decisions,
- contribution history,
- AI routing,
- and approval policy.

Forge executes approved engineering work inside controlled environments.

> **Forge executes. SLAIOS governs.**

## Why It Is an Add-on

Forge should not duplicate:

- identity,
- role management,
- company memory,
- product priorities,
- contribution tracking,
- internal communication,
- or business knowledge.

It consumes those controls from SLAIOS.

## Core Future Responsibilities

- create isolated coding workers,
- clone/check out approved repositories,
- create controlled branches,
- expose safe read/search/edit/test/build/git tools,
- route work to Luna/Terra/Sol through SLAIOS,
- apply structured patches,
- run tests/builds/linters,
- collect QA evidence,
- prepare PRs and engineering reports,
- return timestamps and contribution evidence to SLAIOS.

## Authority Boundary

Forge does not independently:

- deploy production,
- merge consequential changes,
- access unrelated repositories,
- read broad company secrets,
- change payroll/HR data,
- broaden its own scope,
- or override required human review.

## Initial Principle

Start with one worker and one real engineering workflow.

Do not build a large engineering farm before a single controlled worker proves value.
